Welcome to Faith Holiness CDC

Empowering Lives, Transforming Communities

Faith Holiness Community Development Corporation (Faith Holiness CDC) is a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to empowering lives and transforming communities through outreach, support, and faith-based service.

We host free community events that connect youth, men, women, seniors, and families with valuable resources and opportunities. By partnering with community-based organizations, we provide information on housing, employment, education, mental health, non-violence initiatives, and prayer for the community.

Our Mission: To promote hope, love, peace, faith, and overall well-being while strengthening the communities we serve.

About Us

Who We Are

Faith Holiness Community Development Corporation is a nonprofit organization rooted in faith and committed to community empowerment. We believe that every individual deserves access to resources, support, and opportunities to thrive.

Our Vision

To create vibrant, healthy communities where all individuals have access to essential resources and are empowered to reach their full potential.

Our Core Values

  • Hope: We believe in a brighter future for everyone
  • Love: We serve with compassion and care
  • Peace: We promote harmony and non-violence
  • Faith: We are guided by spiritual principles
  • Community: We strengthen bonds through service

What We Do

We serve diverse populations including:

  • Youth and young adults
  • Men
  • Women
  • Seniors
  • Families

Resources & Support

Faith Holiness CDC partners with community-based organizations to provide comprehensive support across multiple areas:

🏠 Housing

Access to housing assistance programs, affordable housing information, and support services to ensure stable living conditions for all community members.

💼 Employment

Job training programs, employment opportunities, resume building, and career counseling to help you achieve economic stability.

📚 Education

Educational resources, scholarship information, tutoring support, and guidance for academic achievement at all levels.

🧠 Mental Health

Mental health resources, counseling referrals, support groups, and wellness programs for emotional well-being.

☮️ Non-Violence

Programs promoting peace, conflict resolution, violence prevention, and community safety initiatives.

🙏 Prayer & Spiritual Support

Faith-based services, prayer groups, spiritual guidance, and pastoral care for the community.

Our Community Outreach

Faith Holiness CDC hosts free community events that bring people together and connect them with valuable resources and opportunities.

Community Gatherings

Regular events where youth, men, women, seniors, and families come together for fellowship, support, and resource sharing.

Youth Programs

Specialized programs designed to empower young people with skills, mentorship, and opportunities for growth.

Women's Initiatives

Programs focused on empowering women through education, employment training, and peer support networks.

Senior Support

Resources and services designed to support our senior community members with dignity and respect.

Family Services

Programs that strengthen families through counseling, educational workshops, and recreational activities.

Men's Mentorship

Mentorship programs for men focused on personal development, leadership, and community involvement.
