# Faith Holiness Community Development Corporation Website Welcome to the official website of Faith Holiness CDC - a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to empowering lives and transforming communities through outreach, support, and faith-based service. ## About Faith Holiness CDC Faith Holiness Community Development Corporation (Faith Holiness CDC) hosts free community events that connect youth, men, women, seniors, and families with valuable resources and opportunities. ### Our Mission To promote hope, love, peace, faith, and overall well-being while strengthening the communities we serve through outreach, support, and faith-based service. ### What We Provide - **Housing Support** - Access to housing assistance programs and affordable housing information - **Employment Resources** - Job training, employment opportunities, and career counseling - **Education** - Educational resources, scholarships, and tutoring support - **Mental Health** - Counseling referrals, support groups, and wellness programs - **Non-Violence Initiatives** - Conflict resolution and community safety programs - **Spiritual Support** - Prayer groups, spiritual guidance, and pastoral care ## Website Features ### Navigation Tabs 1. **Home** - Welcome and mission overview 2. **About Us** - Learn more about our vision, values, and services 3. **Resources** - Explore our comprehensive support services 4. **Community Outreach** - Discover our various programs and initiatives 5. **Photo Gallery** - Visual stories from our community events 6. **Donate** - Support our mission (Zelle donations available) 7. **Contact Us** - Get in touch or send us a message ## File Structure
